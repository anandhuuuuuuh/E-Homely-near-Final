About us

E-Homely is a small home-style kitchen near the college. We cook simple, filling meals the way they are made at home and pack them fresh, so you can skip the queue and still eat well.

Order ahead from your phone, collect at the counter and pay the rest when you pick up.

How it works

  1. Pick a meal and swipe the dishes you want onto your plate.
  2. Enter your name and phone, then pay a small advance online to get your token.
  3. Show your token at the counter any time (it never expires) and pay the rest.
